Common Account Access Problems

Sometimes employees may experience difficulties when trying to access workplace services. These problems can result from incorrect credentials, account restrictions, browser issues or temporary service interruptions.

  • Incorrect credentials: Check that your username and password have been entered correctly.
  • Forgotten password: Follow the authorised account recovery instructions where available.
  • Browser problems: Refresh the page or try another supported browser.
  • Connection issues: Make sure your internet connection is working properly.
  • Account restrictions: Contact the appropriate workplace support team if access remains unavailable.

Simple Ways to Protect Your Account

Employee accounts can contain personal details and workplace information, making account security an important consideration. A few straightforward habits can help reduce the risk of unauthorised access.

  • Create a strong password that is difficult for others to guess.
  • Never share your login credentials with other people.
  • Avoid entering account details on suspicious websites.
  • Always sign out when using a shared computer.
  • Keep your browser and device software updated.
  • Report unusual account activity to the appropriate support team.
